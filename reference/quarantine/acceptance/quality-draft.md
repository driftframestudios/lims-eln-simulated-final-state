# Simulated Quality acceptance packet

**Evidence mode:** simulated  
**Authority:** advisory only  
**Accountable observation required:** yes

The projected evidence chain binds intended use, rules, risks, examples, tests,
environment identity, raw artifacts, deviations, conclusions, and change triggers.
Quality receives a trace matrix plus migration, restore, security, and operational
receipts.

Observed executions and approved procedures replace this forecast at validation.
Quality records the applicable lifecycle controls, training state, deviations,
residual risk, and approval meaning.
