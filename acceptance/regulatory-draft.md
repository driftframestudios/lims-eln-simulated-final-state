# Simulated Regulatory acceptance packet

**Evidence mode:** simulated  
**Authority:** advisory only  
**Accountable observation required:** yes

The projected assessment maps intended use, jurisdiction, record classes, signature
meaning, retention, inspection copies, and applicable predicate rules. Each claim
stays bounded to its named use and environment.

The Regulatory approver determines applicability from the configured system,
organizational procedures, and observed evidence. That determination replaces the
simulation's provisional classification.
